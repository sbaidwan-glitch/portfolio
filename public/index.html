Fall 2025 · Intro to Engineering Design Coursework · Team

Lab Helper — Microcentrifuge Tube Capper

A bench tool that caps a rack of tubes 4.7× faster than by hand.

Our client, a biology lab technician, spent a chunk of every week snapping lids onto microcentrifuge tubes by hand, which is slow and hard on the wrists. For E4 we designed a hinged press that closes a full row at once, paired with a 3D-printed rack that holds the tubes steady and survives ethanol sterilization. Everything had to cost under $30 and not damage the tubes or the user’s gloves.

Result 80 tubes capped in 51 seconds versus about four minutes by hand: 4.7× faster, for under $30.

Team: Samara Baidwan, Sophie, Luna, Soraya, Lucas
