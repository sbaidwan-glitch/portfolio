About

Designing, making, testing.

I’m an engineering student at Harvey Mudd College who enjoys turning ideas into physical hardware — from PCB designs and sensor circuits to machined components.

My experience spans materials research, instrumentation, and underwater robotics, and I’m especially interested in building tools that help us understand and interact with physical systems. I enjoy the process of designing, making, testing, and figuring out why something isn’t working yet.

Summer 2026 Individual research

Carbon-Bronze Alloy Development & Characterization

Investigating how composition and heat treatment affect experimental alloys for bushings and bearings.

During summer research at the University of New South Wales, I developed and characterized 21 experimental copper-based carbon-bronze alloys. My work included calculating compositions, preparing alloy charges, arc melting, heat treatment, metallographic preparation, optical microscopy, and hardness testing. I also improved an existing MATLAB image-analysis pipeline to quantify carbon area fraction and particle-size distributions across compositions and heat treatments.

  • MATLAB
  • Alloy preparation
  • Arc melting
  • Heat treatment
  • Metallography
  • Optical microscopy
  • Hardness testing
Spring 2026 · Experimental Engineering Coursework

Autonomous Surface Vehicle — Environmental Sensing

A calibrated sensor system for collecting environmental measurements during coastal field testing.

I designed a multi-sensor instrumentation system that included a Hall-effect anemometer, magnetic wind-direction sensing, and a photodiode amplifier circuit for measuring solar irradiance. I integrated analog and digital sensors with a Teensy 4.0 and microSD logging, calibrated measurements against reference instruments, and deployed the system on an autonomous surface vehicle. The project was selected for presentation to Harvey Mudd’s Engineering Visitors Committee.

  • Teensy 4.0
  • Hall-effect sensors
  • Photodiodes
  • Op-amp circuits
  • microSD logging
  • Sensor calibration
  • 3D printing

Jan – Dec 2025 Undergraduate research

Fluorescence Characterization & Instrument Troubleshooting

Improving confidence in laboratory measurements through calibration troubleshooting and documentation.

As an undergraduate research assistant in Harvey Mudd’s Molecular Engineering Lab, I conducted fluorescence experiments and investigated circular-dichroism instrument calibration errors. I used hardware inspection and coordination with the manufacturer to diagnose problems, and authored a characterization standard operating procedure. The experience strengthened my interest in reliable instrumentation and reproducible experiments.

  • Fluorescence characterization
  • Circular-dichroism instrumentation
  • Calibration troubleshooting
  • Experimental documentation
